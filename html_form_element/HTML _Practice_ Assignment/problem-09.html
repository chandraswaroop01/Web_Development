Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

All students must carry their college ID card at all times within the campus premises.

Respect faculty, staff, and fellow students. Mutual respect ensures a healthy learning environment.

Attendance

Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory in all subjects to be eligible for examinations.

Attendance will be recorded daily. Medical leave must be supported by valid documents.

Examination

Students must arrive 30 minutes before the exam start time and carry their hall ticket.

Any form of malpractice will result in immediate disqualification and disciplinary action.

Assignment Submission

Assignments must be submitted on or before the deadline. Late submissions beyond 3 days will not be accepted.

Each assignment should include the student’s name, roll number, and department.
Submissions without proper details will be rejected.

Discipline

Use of mobile phones is strictly prohibited inside classrooms and laboratories.

Students must maintain decorum during lectures
