Breaking News: Floods Hit Riverside City

Thousands evacuated as rescue teams work tirelessly

Severe flooding has struck Riverside City after continuous rainfall overwhelmed the riverbanks late last night. Emergency services reported that over 5,000 residents have been evacuated to temporary shelters.

The floods began around midnight, submerging several neighborhoods and cutting off road access. Rescue teams have deployed boats and helicopters to reach stranded families. Officials stressed that public safety remains the highest priority as operations continue.

Government representatives have assured citizens that relief supplies, including food and medical aid, are being distributed. Engineers are working to reinforce embankments, and weather forecasts predict rainfall will ease by September 2.

Campus Update: Annual TechFest Announced

Students gear up for innovation and creativity

The Annual TechFest 2026 has been officially announced by Bright Future University. The three-day event will showcase competitions, workshops, and cultural programs.

Highlights include a hackathon, robotics challenge, and cultural performances. Registration is open online, and students are encouraged to apply early to secure their spots.
